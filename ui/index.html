1. Commit

Sends a hash of the transaction and pays its STX leg. Nothing about which transaction it is leaves your wallet yet.

2. Reveal

Names the transaction and claims its txid, one bitcoin block after the commit. Still nothing broadcast.

3. Send the bitcoin

Only once the reveal has confirmed. To the pool's treasury: —
Never to the staker contract: sBTC arriving there is taken for reward.

4. Wait for the bridge

The sBTC signers sweep the deposit and mint to the treasury. They take their bitcoin fee out of it, so a little less arrives than you sent — the pool credits what arrives.
