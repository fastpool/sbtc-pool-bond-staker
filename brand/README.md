# Esbee

## The name

`sBTC` + `Bond` gives S and B; said aloud, "S-B" is **Esbee**. The pun is the
whole trick: it survives being spoken, which an acronym does not, and it lands
on a real word-shape rather than a string of capitals.

What makes it worth keeping is where it lands. *Esbee* is a bee, and a bee is
already the thing this contract is:

- a **hive** is a membership that acts as one, which is what a DAO claims to be
  and what quadratic voting is trying to make true;
- **workers** put in and the hive holds the store — the pool's members deposit,
  the treasury holds;
- **honey** is yield that accrues slowly and is drawn down later, which is a
  six-month bond almost exactly;
- a **honeycomb cell** is a ready-made mark: geometric, instantly legible at
  16px, and unlike most crypto logos it is not a coin, a shield, or a chain.

Against the alternatives it holds up. `SB DAO` and `S/B` are unpronounceable
and unsearchable. `StackBond` / `BondStack` are literal, generic, and collide
with a dozen things. `Hive`, `Comb` and `Honey` are taken many times over in
this space. `Esbee` is short, spellable after hearing it once, works as a
handle, and reads as a name rather than a claim — no *Protocol*, *Labs*,
*Finance* or *Capital* doing the work.

One caveat worth stating plainly: a search turned up no crypto or DAO project
using the name, which is a first signal and not a clearance. A real trademark
and handle check is still owed before anything is printed on anything.

## The mark

`esbee.svg` — a honeycomb cell whose inner strokes form an S and a B, with a
hint of neighbouring cells above and below, and three tapering bands beneath
that read as a bee once noticed and as nothing at all until then.

The letters are drawn on the cell's own diagonals, so at small sizes the mark
reads as structure and only resolves into initials up close. It carries its own
palette and flips with `prefers-color-scheme`, so it can sit directly on a page
without a plate behind it.

    ink     #1c1a17 / #f2eee6      the comb
    accent  #d1620a / #f08a3c      the letters — bitcoin orange, warmed
